# Global Electronic Retail Store Sales & Customer Analysis

## Project Background

This project analyzes the sales performance of a fictitious global electronics retailer using transactional, customer, product, store, and currency exchange data.

The objective of the analysis is to identify key business trends, evaluate customer behavior, measure product and market performance, and uncover actionable business insights through interactive Excel dashboards.

---

# Executive Summary

## Overview of Findings

Analysis of **55.8M** in revenue, **26,326 orders**, and **15,266 customers** revealed strong business growth from **2016 to 2019**, followed by a sharp decline of approximately **50%** in both revenue and orders during **2020**.

Despite this downturn, delivery performance remained stable and continued to improve, suggesting that operational efficiency was **not responsible** for the decline.

The **Computers** category emerged as the company's strongest business segment, leading in revenue, profit, customers, and orders. **Home Appliances**, although generating fewer orders, achieved the highest **Average Order Value (AOV)**, highlighting its premium positioning.

The **United States** remained the dominant market, contributing the largest share of revenue, profit, stores, and active customers.

Customer analysis showed that the **31–60** age group generated the highest revenue, while **61.2%** of active customers placed more than one order, indicating strong repeat purchasing behavior.

---

# Revenue & Profit Insights

- **Category Performance:** Computers generated the highest revenue (**19.3M**) and profit (**11.3M**), making it the company's strongest-performing category. Home Appliances ranked second in profitability despite handling fewer orders.

- **Average Order Value:** Home Appliances recorded the highest AOV, followed by TV & Video and Computers, indicating customers are willing to spend significantly more on premium electronic products.

- **Lowest Performing Category:** Games & Toys recorded the lowest revenue, profit, and AOV despite serving a reasonable customer base, suggesting weaker monetization compared to other categories.

- **Country Performance:** The United States generated **29.9M** in revenue and **17.5M** in profit, contributing nearly half of overall business performance. France remained the weakest-performing major market despite operating seven stores.

- **Yearly Trend:** Revenue and profit increased consistently between **2016 and 2019**, peaking in **2019** before declining by approximately **50%** in **2020**.

- **Top Products:** High-revenue products were dominated by desktop computer models, reinforcing the Computers category as the company's primary revenue driver.

---

# Customer Insights

- **Customer Distribution:** The **31–60** age group contributed the highest revenue and order volume, while the **18–30** segment generated the lowest.

- **Gender Analysis:** Male customers generated slightly higher revenue and orders overall. However, females contributed more revenue in **Music, Movies & Audiobooks** and placed more orders in **Cameras & Camcorders** and **Music, Movies & Audiobooks**.

- **Category Popularity:** Computers attracted the highest number of customers, generated the highest sales, and processed the largest order volume, making it the company's most broadly successful category.

- **Customer Loyalty:** Approximately **61.2%** of active customers placed multiple orders, indicating strong repeat purchasing behavior among engaged customers.

- **Active Customers:** Significant differences between total and active customers were observed across countries. Australia and France showed comparatively high proportions of inactive customers, indicating opportunities for customer re-engagement.

---

# Market & Geographic Insights

- The **United States** remained the company's largest market across nearly every business metric, including revenue, profit, orders, stores, and active customers.

- The **United Kingdom, Germany, and Canada** formed the next tier of high-performing markets.

- Despite having only **three stores**, Italy generated substantially higher revenue than France, which operated **seven stores**, indicating significantly stronger store productivity.

- France consistently underperformed across revenue, profit, and store performance despite maintaining a moderate store network.

---

# Sales Trends & Seasonality

- Revenue displayed clear seasonality, with **December, February, and January** consistently emerging as the strongest sales months.

- **April** remained the weakest-performing month across nearly every year, suggesting a recurring seasonal slowdown.

- Business growth between **2016 and 2019** occurred consistently across nearly all months rather than being concentrated in specific periods.

- Although revenue declined sharply in **2020**, the seasonal purchasing pattern remained largely unchanged, indicating reduced overall demand rather than a shift in customer purchasing behavior.

- The approximately **50% decline** observed in **2020** occurred consistently across categories and countries, suggesting a company-wide downturn rather than problems within specific markets or product lines.

---

# Store Performance Insights

- Physical stores generated approximately **80%** of total revenue, while online sales contributed the remaining **20%**.

- The United States contained the majority of the company's highest-performing stores.

- Most of the lowest-performing stores were located in France, reinforcing the country's weaker market performance.

- Larger stores generally generated higher revenue than smaller stores. A moderate positive correlation (**r = 0.60**) between store area and sales suggests that larger retail space is associated with stronger revenue generation.

- Nine stores recorded **zero sales** despite being operational for several years. Since these stores are located within otherwise high-performing markets such as the **United States, Canada, Germany, and Australia**, the issue appears to be store-specific rather than country-specific.

---

# Operational Insights

- Average delivery time remained remarkably consistent at approximately **4.5 days** across months, countries, and product categories.

- Delivery performance improved steadily from **2016 through 2021**, indicating continuous improvements in operational efficiency.

- Because delivery times continued improving even during the sharp revenue decline in **2020**, there is no evidence that delivery performance contributed to the downturn in sales.

# Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following business recommendations are proposed:

- **Strengthen High-Performing Categories:** Continue investing in high-performing categories such as **Computers** and **Home Appliances**, which consistently generated the highest revenue, profit, and average order value. Expanding premium product offerings and promoting best-selling products could further increase profitability.

- **Improve Underperforming Categories:** Categories such as **Games & Toys** and **Audio** generated comparatively lower revenue and profit. Conduct further market research to understand customer preferences and evaluate product assortment, pricing, or promotional strategies to improve performance.

- **Focus on the French Market:** France consistently underperformed despite having a moderate number of stores. Review regional marketing strategies, product mix, and store operations to identify opportunities for improving sales performance.

- **Investigate Zero-Sales Stores:** Several stores recorded no sales despite being operational for many years. These locations should be audited to determine whether the issue is related to operational inefficiencies, inventory availability, reporting errors, or local market conditions.

- **Leverage Customer Loyalty:** Approximately **61% of active customers** made repeat purchases, indicating strong customer loyalty. Introducing loyalty programs, personalized recommendations, and targeted promotions could encourage even higher repeat purchase rates.

- **Capitalize on Seasonal Demand:** Sales consistently peaked during **December, January, and February**. Increasing inventory levels, promotional campaigns, and staffing ahead of these peak periods could help maximize revenue opportunities.

- **Address Seasonal Slowdowns:** Since **April** consistently recorded the lowest sales across multiple years, targeted seasonal campaigns or promotional events could help stimulate demand during this traditionally weaker period.

- **Expand Successful Store Practices:** The highest-performing stores were largely concentrated in the United States. Analyzing the operational practices, product assortment, and customer engagement strategies of these stores may provide insights that can be applied to lower-performing locations.

- **Continue Maintaining Delivery Performance:** Delivery times remained consistently around **4.5 days** and improved over the years. Maintaining this operational efficiency should remain a priority, as reliable delivery contributes positively to the customer experience.
